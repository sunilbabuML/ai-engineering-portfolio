## Monthly Maintenance Fee
The monthly maintenance fee for a savings account is $5.

## Fee Waiver
The monthly maintenance fee is waived when the account maintains an average daily balance of at least $300.

## Interest Rate
Savings accounts earn an annual percentage yield (APY) of 2.50%.

## Withdrawals
Customers may make up to 6 withdrawals per monthly statement cycle without an additional withdrawal fee.

Each additional withdrawal beyond 6 incurs a $10 fee.

## Minimum Opening Deposit
The minimum opening deposit for a savings account is $100.
