## ATM Withdrawal Limits
The daily ATM withdrawal limit for checking accounts is $500.

The daily ATM withdrawal limit for savings accounts is $300.

## Debit Card Purchase Limits
The daily debit card purchase limit for checking accounts is $2,500.

## Internal Transfers
Customers may transfer up to $10,000 per day between their own accounts.

Internal transfers between accounts belonging to the same customer do not incur a transfer fee.

## External Transfers
The daily external transfer limit is $5,000.

Standard external transfers take 1 to 3 business days to complete.

## Wire Transfers
Domestic wire transfers have a daily limit of $25,000.

The outgoing domestic wire transfer fee is $20.

International wire transfers require additional verification.

## Security Restrictions
Transactions exceeding the applicable daily limits will be declined.

Suspicious transactions may be temporarily held for fraud review.

Customers may request a limit increase by contacting customer support, subject to verification and approval.
